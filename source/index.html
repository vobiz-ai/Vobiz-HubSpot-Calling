    Frequently asked questions

    How does incoming call pop-up work?

    When a customer dials your VoBiz number, an incoming call pop-up appears immediately on your screen with caller details. You can click Answer to pick up or Decline to dismiss.

    Why is the Call button disabled?

    Calling requires both a signed-in VoBiz account and a live SIP registration. The hint under the button indicates which one is pending.

    Do I need a headset?

    Audio streams directly via WebRTC through your microphone and speakers. Be sure to grant microphone access in your browser when prompted.

    Where do recordings go?

    VoBiz records the conversation and displays recent files in the recordings list above for instant playback and CRM tracking.
